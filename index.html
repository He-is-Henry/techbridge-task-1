About TechBridge

Technology, practical learning and real-world skills

TechBridge Services is a technology-focused initiative under Baselink Services Limited, created to bridge the gap between technology, businesses and people. We focus on technology services, digital solutions, practical learning, and helping individuals develop skills that can be applied in real-world situations.

Who it's for

Beginners who want real-world experience and a structured way to start building their skills.

What you'll do

Work on practical, project-based tasks in your chosen track and receive feedback along the way.

How it runs

The program is remote. Tasks, resources and announcements are shared through the TechBridge community.

The Internship

A 30-day practical internship

Real tasks, real deadlines and real feedback. The TechBridge internship is designed to help you learn by doing and finish with work you can show.

Start your application
30days
  • Practical, project-based tasks in your chosen track
  • Feedback and corrections to help you improve
  • Remote and free to join
  • Recommendation letter for interns who successfully meet the program requirements
  • Certificate of Completion for interns who meet the completion requirements

Completion benefits are based on meeting the program requirements. They are not automatic.
